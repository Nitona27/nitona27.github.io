Quick check before sending

  • No clipping (distortion)
  • All files start at the same pointAligned to the top
  • File format: wav 48kHz/24bit
  • No overlapping voices in one fileSplit each part into its own file
  • You have permission to use the instrumental
  • File names in English letters onlyAvoids broken characters

06

Recording quality & result

The final sound depends on your recording. I always do my best, but this is what you can expect:

Quiet room, no clipping
★★★
Polished, release-ready sound
Some room echo or background noise
★★
Clean and balanced, but a little room sound may remain
Clipping, loud noise, phone mic
★
Only limited improvement. I may ask you to re-record

If the quality is too low to mix (heavy clipping, loud noise and so on), I'll ask you to re-record before I start.
